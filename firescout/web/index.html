FireScout scout robot

Placed at the doorway by the first crew while BA is donned. Searches ahead in zero visibility and tells the Incident Commander where people are, how urgent each one is, where the fire is and which way is safe.

Thermal cameraLWIR 160x120, 57 deg (FLIR Lepton 3.5) - finds bodies + fire through smoke
60 GHz radar360 deg ranging for mapping walls; breathing micro-motion to 3 m
Depth + IR night camera850 nm illuminator; tells furniture from walls by object height
4-mic arrayhears calls for help, gives direction of the voice
Speakertalks to people it finds: stay low, stay put, or which way is out
Gas cellsCO + O2 (4-gas monitor cells) - marks air that kills
Self temperatureretreats before electronics cook (60 C limit)
Mesh radiokeeps working with no link, uploads when back in range
